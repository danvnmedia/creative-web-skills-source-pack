## What makes a 3D showcase useful

Do not rate references only by visual spectacle. Evaluate:

- Does 3D explain a product/spatial relationship?
- Does the camera have readable beats?
- Is DOM content still usable?
- Is interaction discoverable?
- Does the experience survive mobile/reduced motion?
- Could a simpler 2D method communicate the same idea better?
