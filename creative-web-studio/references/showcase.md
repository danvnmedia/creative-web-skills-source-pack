## How to use a showcase

For each reference, extract only:

1. **Narrative role** — why the interaction exists.
2. **Composition principle** — what is visually dominant.
3. **Motion grammar** — repeated transition/interaction motifs.
4. **Technical hypothesis** — likely DOM/CSS/canvas/WebGL split.
5. **Transfer rule** — how to adapt the principle to the user's subject.
6. **Difference rule** — what must materially change to keep the result original.

Never say “make it exactly like X.” Convert X into 2–4 reusable principles and combine them with the user's own content and metaphor.
